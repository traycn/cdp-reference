## Wrong attachment and the floor

**Floor:** events produced by someone other than the device's owner, in a session with no form fill, ÷ events whose device is attachable. Nothing in such a session says who produced it, so any rule that attaches it can only guess.

**The floor is a lower bound** on the unavoidable part, not all of it. It counts borrowers' events only. An owner's session without a form fill, on a device where only the borrower filled forms, is just as unidentifiable and is not counted. The floor is above the 0.010 bar on every seed, so the bar is reported as a miss; see [ADR 0004](adr/0004-device-attachment.md).

## Same-name relatives with both DOBs present

The bar counts a pair as merged when any unit of one relative shares a profile with any unit of the other. The breakdown separates the two ways that happens:

- **Contact to contact:** both relatives' CRM contacts in one profile. 01 `v1.1.0` makes a DOB mismatch a clustering cannot-link, so this is expected to be 0.
- **Only through form fills:** the contacts are apart, but a form fill of one relative sits with the other relative's contact. Forms carry no DOB; a form with the same name and the relatives' shared identifier matches both contacts, and nothing on it says which relative filled it. See [ADR 0001](adr/0001-reuse-entity-resolution.md#consequences).
